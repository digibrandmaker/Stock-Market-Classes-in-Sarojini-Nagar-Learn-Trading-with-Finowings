## Why Learn the Stock Market?

The stock market allows investors to buy and sell shares of publicly listed companies. However, understanding market movements requires knowledge, practice, and disciplined decision-making. Beginners should learn how stock exchanges work, what influences share prices, and how to evaluate investment opportunities before making financial decisions.

## What Can You Learn in Stock Market Classes?

A structured stock market learning programme may cover several important topics, including:

- **Stock Market Basics:** Understand shares, stock exchanges, indices, and demat accounts.
- **Technical Analysis:** Learn candlestick patterns, charts, support, resistance, and market trends.
- **Fundamental Analysis:** Understand company financial statements, earnings, and business performance.
- **Trading Strategies:** Explore intraday trading, swing trading, and positional trading concepts.
- **Risk Management:** Learn about stop-loss orders, position sizing, and risk-reward ratios.
- **Trading Psychology:** Develop discipline and understand the importance of controlling emotions.

These concepts can help learners build a foundation for understanding the financial markets.
